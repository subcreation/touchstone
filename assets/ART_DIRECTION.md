# Portfolio Art Direction

Status: **final artwork selected; all four heroes are in place**.

The four skill heroes should feel like one family without flattening the
meaning of each object. The recommended starting direction is a stylized
editorial product portrait rather than literal product photography: one tactile
object, a bold silhouette, a consistent camera angle, restrained texture, and
generous negative space.

This gives the Luminol image room for a deep indigo and cyan black-light scene
without forcing the other three images into photorealism. Shared composition,
surface texture, shadow shape, and typographic restraint can unify the set even
when each image uses a different dominant color.

## Final Set

- **Floss:** a hand-drawn pastel editorial illustration (Midjourney) of a
  person flossing, in close-up, chosen for warmth and charm over a product shot.
- **Touchstone:** a photoreal macro of fingers drawing a gold bar across a
  black touchstone, leaving a gold streak.
- **Luminol:** a photoreal bootprint revealed glowing blue under luminol on a
  dark floor, used unedited to keep its contrast.
- **Throughline:** a pastel ink illustration of a hand with a marker poised
  over a blank sticky note at the end of a receding row of filled notes, matched
  in style to Floss.

Two heroes are illustrated (Floss, Throughline) and two are photoreal
(Touchstone, Luminol); the set is unified by tight close-ups of a hand-scale
physical metaphor. Banners are committed at their native generated size, and the
same file serves as both `hero-dark.png` and `hero-light.png`.

## Visual Anchors

These concept anchors guided exploration; the Final Set above records where
the finished art departed from them.

- **Floss:** a classic waxed-floss dispenser, unbranded, with a single taut
  strand making the handoff idea visible.
- **Luminol:** a boot print fluorescing under black light, with indigo darkness
  and an electric cyan evidence glow.
- **Touchstone:** a small gold bar leaving a fresh mark across a dark assay
  stone; the proof is in the visible streak.
- **Throughline:** a broad black marker drawing one decisive, uninterrupted
  line across a yellow note.

## Shared Constraints

- Use the same aspect ratio, object scale, viewpoint family, and margin system.
- Prefer a memorable editorial silhouette over generic stock realism.
- Let one accent color dominate each image while retaining a shared neutral
  palette and grain or paper texture.
- Keep generated lettering, logos, trademarks, and product branding out of the
  art.
- Compose for both light and dark README contexts and for a narrow mobile crop.
- Deliver final README artwork as PNG, JPEG, or WebP according to the selected
  visual style. SVG is not required.
- Preserve layered or high-resolution source files so details can be redrawn.
- Finish all four as a set before declaring any one image final.

## Exploration Workflow

1. Collect reference boards for the objects, lighting, composition, and one
   candidate editorial style.
2. Use Midjourney for rapid set-level style exploration, not final text or
   brand marks.
3. Bring the strongest composition into Krita or ComfyUI for controlled redraw,
   inpainting, and consistency work. Use ControlNet or simple 3D blockouts only
   when perspective or object continuity is otherwise expensive.
4. Complete the final drawing pass by hand where generated details weaken the
   silhouette or the meaning.

Timebox the shared style decision before polishing individual images. The
final set was produced together, and the committed `hero-light.png` and
`hero-dark.png` files are the final portfolio artwork.
